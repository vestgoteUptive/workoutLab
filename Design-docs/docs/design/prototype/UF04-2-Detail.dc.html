Back Squat

Depth
Hip crease at or below the knee
Bar
Stays over mid-foot
Knees
Track in line with toes
Placeholder illustration. Swap for a looped animation.
Muscles worked
Quadriceps Glutes Adductors Hamstrings Spinal erectors Core
Highlighted = primary. Grey = secondary or stabiliser.
How to
1Set the bar on your upper back, feet about shoulder-width, toes slightly out.
2Take a big breath and brace your core before you move.
3Sit down between your hips, knees pushing out over your toes, until thighs reach parallel or lower.
4Drive through your whole foot and stand up, keeping chest and hips rising together.
Common mistakes
Knees caving in · heels lifting · hips shooting up first · cutting depth short
What the research says
Over 10 weeks, full-depth squats grew the glutes and adductors more than half squats, while quad growth was similar. Neither grew the hamstrings much.
Kubo et al., 2019 · Eur J Appl Physiol
