workout LAB

Train every muscle group, in the time you have

Say how many minutes you have. workout LAB plans the session that best evens out your last two weeks, and shows one step at a time.

Open the app

Free. Works in your browser, and installs to your home screen.

How it works

Balance you can see

Every muscle area gets a score from your weighted hard sets over the last 14 days, so you see what is ahead and what is lagging.

Fits your time

Start by saying how many minutes you have. The plan is built to fit that time.

One step at a time

During a workout you see one thing: the current set or rest. Everything else waits behind Pause.

Targets that adapt

Suggestions come from clear, fixed rules, and your targets shift with what you actually train when you accept a check-in.

Your data stays yours

We store only your email, your training and your plan settings, hosted in the EU. No third-party analytics, no trackers, no ads. Export or delete everything in the app.
