Back Squat.
Illustration, looped
Depth
Hip crease at or below the knee
Bar
Stays over mid-foot
Knees
Track in line with toes
Muscles worked
QuadricepsGlutesAdductorsHamstringsSpinal erectorsCore
Filled = primary. Outline = secondary or stabiliser.
How to
1Set the bar on your upper back, feet about shoulder-width, toes slightly out.
2Take a big breath and brace your core before you move.
3Sit down between your hips, knees pushing out over your toes, until thighs reach parallel or lower.
4Drive through your whole foot and stand up, keeping chest and hips rising together.

Today · what's wrong
Charcoal + lime + condensed caps is the default output of every fitness template. It reads generic because it is.
Rounded cards inside rounded cards, an icon in a circle, label-over-big-number stat tiles, a pill CTA. None of it is wrong; none of it is yours. Below are five directions. None of them is "light mode" or "dark mode": each picks a mid-tone ground and commits to it.
1aClay — editorial
Terracotta ground, a serif for words, mono for numbers. Feels like a training journal, not a gadget. No cards: hairline rules do the structure. Most opinionated choice: the CTA is a flat bar, not a glowing pill.
