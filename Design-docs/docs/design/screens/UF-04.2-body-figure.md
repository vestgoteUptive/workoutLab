- The figure card replaces the two bare pill lists at the same spot (under the tagline). The lists move **into** the card, under the figure.
- The figure is centred, 200 px tall, and its width follows the viewBox (about 177 px). The card has no title. The labels name what the fills mean.

## Fills

| Area weight (`exercise.areas[a]`) | Region style | Swatch in the list label |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 (primary) | Solid `accent` | 12 × 12 px solid `accent`, radius 3 |
| 0.5 (secondary) | `accent` diagonal hatch, 1.5 px stripes at 45°, 5 px period, on `surface-2` | 12 × 12 px, the same hatch, 1 px `text-muted` border |
| absent | `surface-2` | — |

- Every region has a 1 px `text-muted` border. The body outline is 1.5 px `text-muted`. Seams inside `accent` regions use `on-accent`, and elsewhere `line-strong` (decorative).
- An area that appears in both views (shoulders, arms) is filled in both.
- The fills come only from `exercise.areas`. Nothing is computed beyond reading 1.0 or 0.5.

## List labels

- Above each pill list there's a visible label: **"Primary"** and **"Secondary"** (card label style), followed by the swatch. The swatch is `aria-hidden`.
- The lists keep their accessible names: "Primary areas" and "Secondary areas" (`en.uf04.primaryAreas` / `secondaryAreas`). The visible word starts the name (WCAG 2.5.3).
- No secondary areas: the Secondary label and list are not rendered. The hatch is then not on screen either, so no legend for it is needed.
- The pills are unchanged: text, area names in the fixed order (engine rule 1).

## Accessibility

- The `<svg>` is `aria-hidden="true"` and not focusable. The text equivalent is the two named lists directly under it.
- Primary and secondary differ by **pattern** (solid versus hatched) as well as by the list text. Contrast: `accent` against `surface` is 13.5:1, against `surface-2` 11.5:1, and the hatch stripes against their ground 11.5:1. The region border `text-muted` is 6.8:1 on `surface`.
- No new controls, so no new targets. The tab order is unchanged.
- Forced colours: primary `CanvasText` fill, secondary `CanvasText` hatch, borders `CanvasText`.
- Text zoom 200 %: the pills wrap. The figure keeps its size.
