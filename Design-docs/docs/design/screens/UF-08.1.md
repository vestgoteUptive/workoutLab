# UF-08.1 Time & energy

- **Screen:** UF-08.1 Time & energy, route `/session/setup` (also `?step=time`; any other cold `step` lands here, D-0107 §1). Entry: Start on UF-02.1 or UF-02.2, and "Quick 20-min" on UF-02.1 (opens with 20 minutes chosen, D-0217). No tab bar (D-0196). It asks how long the user has before every workout (principle 2).
- **Decisions:** D-0065 §2–§3 (stepper, chips, finish time, fit line), D-0107 (build defaults: loading, copy, clock, offline icon, no-profile state), D-0191 §1 (Skip today), D-0236 §1 ("Start empty"), D-0239 §16 (this spec), D-0203 (visual foundation), D-0208 and D-0211 (Cobalt). Spec: `docs/specs/empty-start-plan.md` (UF-08.1).
- **Look:** Cobalt + state colour (D-0208); state `plan`; patterns in `components/state-patterns.md` (Primary button, Secondary button, Text button, Chip, Segmented control, Toggle, Input).
- **Foundation classes (D-0203):** `.wl-page`, `.wl-card`, `.wl-chip`, `.wl-segmented`, `.wl-toggle`, `.wl-input`, `.wl-button--primary`, `.wl-button--secondary`, `.wl-button--text`.
- **Type:** Familjen Grotesk through `--wl-font` (the plan font, `--wl-font-plan`). The minutes value uses the hero role with tabular figures; everything else uses the plan roles in `design-system.md` (sentence case, no uppercase transforms).
- **Tokens:** existing `--wl-` variables only: `--wl-bg`, `--wl-raise`, `--wl-line`, `--wl-ink`, `--wl-ink-muted`, `--wl-selected`, `--wl-on-selected`, `--wl-action`, `--wl-on-action`, `--wl-focus`. No new token, no raw colour value.
- **Components:** none new. This spec documents the screen as built (T-0303a, T-0520, T-0599) and adds one control, "Start empty".

## Layout (top to bottom, 320 px and 390 px wide)

The root is `.wl-page` with `data-wl-state="plan"` and `data-screen-id="UF-08.1"`, 16 px between blocks. Nothing scrolls sideways at 320 px; long copy wraps.

1. **Top bar:** **Close** (×, `aria-hidden` icon, accessible name "Close", at least 44 × 44 px, top left) leaves setup for `/`. The offline icon `OfflineStatus variant="icon"` sits top right (D-0107 §8).
2. **Title:** `<h1>` "How long do you have?" (page title role).
3. **Time card** (`.wl-card`):
   1. **Minutes stepper:** "−" and "+" outline pill buttons (56 × 56 px, 1.5 px `--wl-ink` outline), accessible names "5 minutes less" and "5 minutes more". Steps of 5 within 15–120; default 45 (D-0065 §2). At 15 the "−" and at 120 the "+" are `aria-disabled="true"` (`--wl-ink-muted` outline and text) and a tap does nothing. Between them: the minutes value (hero, tabular figures), the unit "minutes" and the line "done by HH:MM" (label role, `--wl-ink-muted`). The clock is read once on mount (D-0107 §4).
   2. **Quick chips:** a `role="group"` named "Quick picks" with five `.wl-chip` buttons 20 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 in one row, each with `aria-pressed` and the accessible name "{n} minutes", at least 44 × 44 px. The chip matching the minutes is on.
   3. **Finish time:** a "Set a finish time" text button. Tapped, it becomes an `<input type="time">` labelled "Finish by" (`.wl-input`, 48 px). A complete valid time sets the minutes and closes the input; a rejected one keeps it open with "Pick a time later today" (`aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`) and leaves the minutes unchanged (D-0107 §6).
   4. **Warm-up toggle:** `.wl-toggle` row "Warm-up counts in this time (3 min)" on a native checkbox, on by default, between two 1 px `--wl-line` hairlines.
4. **Energy:** a `role="radiogroup"` labelled "Energy" (`.wl-label` text) with a three-part `.wl-segmented` control on native radios: Low / Normal / High, Normal checked by default. The hint under it (`--wl-ink-muted`, also the group's `aria-describedby`) follows the choice: "Fewer sets, same weights." / "Your plan as written." / "Adds a back-off set to the main lift if time allows."
5. **Skip today** (D-0191 §1): a `role="group"` labelled "Skip today" with the hint "Sore or busy? Skipped areas stay out of this workout." and nine `.wl-chip` toggles in the fixed area order (chest, back, shoulders, arms, core, glutes, quads, hamstrings, calves), `aria-pressed`, none pressed by default, each at least 44 × 44 px. A pressed chip shows the 2 px stroke tick (`aria-hidden`) before the label, so the state is not shown by fill alone. The chips wrap; for this workout only.
6. **Live fit line:** "Fits: {n} {exercise|exercises}, {m} {set|sets} + warm-up" (stat-like, 1.375 rem, weight 600), "Nothing fits in {n} min" at 0, and "Checking what fits…" before the first result (D-0107 §5, §7). `aria-live="polite"`. It sits above the buttons (`margin-top: auto`, so the buttons sit at the bottom of a tall screen). It describes the full suggestion in both cases; "Start empty" does not change it.
7. **"Suggest my workout"**: `.wl-button--primary`, full width, the arrow icon (`aria-hidden`) right-aligned. The one primary action on this screen and the default path (onboarding and Quick 20-min are unchanged, principle 5). Opens UF-08.2 in suggested mode.
8. **"Start empty"** (D-0236 §1): see below.

## "Start empty" (D-0236 §1, D-0239 §16)

- **Position:** directly **under "Suggest my workout"**, 12 px gap, the last control on the screen. Nothing sits between the two buttons.
- **Treatment:** the Cobalt **secondary button** (`.wl-button--secondary`): a transparent pill with a 1.5 px `--wl-ink` outline and `--wl-ink` text, 1.125 rem weight 600, no icon. **Same width as the primary** (full width of the content column). It is never the primary: "Suggest my workout" stays the only `--wl-action` fill on the screen.
- **Target:** at least 44 px tall and wide (WCAG 2.5.8); build it at least 48 px tall, the secondary button's form minimum (16 px vertical padding).
- **Label:** "Start empty". The visible text is the accessible name; no `aria-haspopup` (it navigates, like the primary).
- **Action:** uses the same minutes, warm-up toggle, Energy and Skip today as the primary, and opens UF-08.2 in **empty mode** for this visit (`mode: "empty"`, `addedIds: []`, `mainPickId: null`; D-0236 §2, D-0239 §1). Not remembered: the next visit starts with both buttons as here (D-0236 revisit).
- **Disabled:** follows the primary. Before the first engine result ("Checking what fits…") both are `aria-disabled="true"` with `aria-describedby` → the fit line, stay focusable, and a tap does nothing. Disabled look: `--wl-ink-muted` outline and text (5.9:1 on `plan.bg`).
- **Hover / focus:** hover thickens the outline to 2 px; focus-visible 2 px `--wl-focus` ring at a 2 px offset.

## States

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Loading (no result yet) | Every control; fit line "Checking what fits…"; both buttons `aria-disabled` |
| Ready | Fit line "Fits: …"; both buttons enabled |
| Nothing fits | "Nothing fits in {n} min"; both buttons enabled (UF-08.2 explains) |
| Finish time open / rejected | The time input; "Pick a time later today" under it when rejected |
| No profile or fewer than 9 targets | Title, then "Connect to finish setting up your plan" and a "Back to Today" link; no time controls, no buttons (D-0107 §9) |
| Offline | Identical; the offline icon in the top bar. `suggest` runs on the device (D-0063 §3), so both buttons work offline |

## Focus

- Tab order follows the layout: Close, stepper −, +, the five chips, "Set a finish time" (or the time input), warm-up toggle, Energy radios, Skip today chips, "Suggest my workout", "Start empty".
- Opening "Set a finish time" moves focus into the time input; a valid time returns focus to "Set a finish time".
- Back from UF-08.2 lands here with the inputs kept and the visit's mode dropped (D-0109 §1).

## Accessibility

- Targets at least 44 × 44 px everywhere (WCAG 2.5.8); the steppers are 56 px, the primary at least 54 px and "Start empty" at least 48 px tall.
- Contrast: `--wl-ink` on `plan.bg` 8.6:1; `--wl-ink-muted` on `plan.bg` 5.9:1; `plan.on-action` on `plan.action` 8.6:1; the secondary outline meets 3:1 (non-text).
- Focus ring 2 px `--wl-focus`, offset 2 px, never removed.
- State by text and attributes, never colour alone: `aria-pressed` chips with a tick, native radios, a native checkbox, `aria-disabled` with a reason line.
- One live region: the fit line (`aria-live="polite"`).
- This is UF-08 (before the workout). No UF-09 control is added.
- Build: web-feature:UF-08 (T-0940 adds "Start empty").
