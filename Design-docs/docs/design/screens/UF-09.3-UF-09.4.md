- **Principle 1:** the only change in focus mode is the text of the reason line. There is no banner, modal or link.

## Weight stepper

The weight stepper in UF-09.4 steps by the exercise's step: the user's own step for that exercise, or the library default. The pre-filled weight comes from the engine and already follows the same step.

## Reason line

The reason line explains the pre-filled weight in one short sentence. The `prefill` reason kinds are:

| Kind | Copy |
|---|---|
| `increase` | Unchanged |
| `hold_effort` | Same weight again. Last time was close to failure. Leave a couple of reps in reserve and it goes up. |

- `hold_effort` has no placeholders: the engine outputs no reps or weight with it, so the text depends on the kind alone.
- It uses the existing reason-line style, position and size, and wraps to as many lines as needed at 200 % text.
- It is plain text in the reason line: no banner, modal or link, and no other change in focus mode.
