## Area row

A row is, top to bottom (all text wraps; the row grows, never clips):

1. **Open button** (a `<button>`, full row width, at least 64 px tall, the row's only button except the stepper). Line 1: the area name (row title role, `--wl-ink`) and, after it on the same line, the time cost "~14 min" (`--wl-ink-muted`). Line 2: "{n} exercises" (`--wl-ink-muted`; "1 exercise" for one) at the left and a chevron (2 px stroke, `aria-hidden`) at the right. The whole block opens UF-08.6 for this area (`?step=area&area=chest`). Accessible name: "{Area}, ~{m} min, {n} exercises", which starts with the visible name (WCAG 2.5.3). The exercise names are **not** on the row (D-0274: exercises are a detail you open only if you want to).
2. **Stepper**, a `role="group"` named "Sets for {Area}" and laid out as `−  6 sets  +`, centred in the row, 8 px between its parts:
   - **−** and **+** are outline pill buttons (`--wl-ink` outline 1.5 px, `--wl-ink` icon 2 px stroke, `aria-hidden`), **48 × 48 px** (the UF-08.1 minutes stepper's buttons at 48 px). Accessible names "One set less for {Area}" and "One set more for {Area}".
   - The value "6 sets" ("2 sets" at the minimum; "1 set" never occurs) is text in the middle, weight 600, tabular figures, at least 72 px wide, `--wl-ink`. The group is 48 + 72 + 48 + 16 = 184 px, so it fits at 264 px. It is not a control and not a focus stop.
   - At the 2-set minimum **−** becomes **Remove**: a pill with the visible text "Remove" (at least 96 × 48 px, same outline), accessible name "Remove {Area}". The stepper is then 96 + 72 + 48 + 16 = 232 px, which still fits in 264 px. At 200 % zoom the group may wrap the value under the buttons; nothing clips.
3. **Refusal line** (`--wl-ink-muted` on `--wl-bg` 5.9:1, directly under the stepper, same id as the **+** button's `aria-describedby`): empty and absent until a **+** is refused, then it holds the reason and stays until the next tap in this row (see "Refusals").
4. **History line:** "Last 14 days: 8 of 20 sets" (`load of target`, from `balance()`, so it works offline; `--wl-ink-muted`). Zero history reads "Last 14 days: 0 of 20 sets". There is no attention colour or outline here (UF-10 owns that).

Labels are unique per row (the area is in every name). State is words ("6 sets", "Remove", the refusal line), never colour or position alone.

## The stepper rules (D-0275 §3)

- **+** adds one set: the engine adds it to the area's first exercise with room, else adds a second exercise for the area at 2 sets, else refuses. A main lift holds at most 4 sets, any other exercise 3, an area at most 2 exercises, so an area holds **2 to 7 sets: 7 for the area with the main lift, 6 for any other**.
- **−** removes one set (the last exercise with more than 2 sets loses one; else the area's second exercise goes). At the 2-set minimum **−** is **Remove**: one tap takes the area out for this visit. It joins this visit's skipped areas (D-0191, so a later rebuild does not bring it back; the "Skipping today" line names it), moves to "Add an area" where one tap puts it back, and there is **no confirm**. The freed time stays unused.
- **+ refused (time or cap):** the button stays focusable, gets `aria-disabled="true"` (dimmed: `--wl-ink-muted` outline and icon, 5.9:1) and a tap does nothing but show the reason in the row's refusal line:
  - time: "No time for another set. Pick more time or remove a set elsewhere."
  - cap (the area holds its maximum): "{Area} already has the most sets it can hold."
  - over budget (after a UF-08.3 swap that did not fit): "Your workout is {over} min over. Remove or swap an exercise first." (see state "Over budget").
  - Check order is the engine's: cap, then time. Never over budget through a **+**.
- The refusal also goes to the status line, so it is read once. The tap that was refused leaves focus on the same **+**.
- The `aria-disabled` look is applied only while the engine would refuse; it is computed from the plan, so after a time chip or a **−** elsewhere it clears without a tap.

## Add an area

- **Container:** a native `details` named by its `summary` **"Add an area"** (at least 48 px tall, chevron 2 px stroke `aria-hidden`), **collapsed** when the plan has areas, **open and the main content** when the plan has none (Start empty, everything skipped). Opening it is not an edit.
- **Rows** (`.wl-row`, at least 64 px tall, wrap): the areas not in the plan, ordered by the engine's `areaCandidates` (lowest projected coverage first, ties in the fixed area order). Line 1: the area (row title). Line 2: "Last 14 days: 0 of 20 sets" (`--wl-ink-muted`). Then the **Add** button, `.wl-button--secondary` (`--wl-ink` outline), visible text "Add", accessible name "Add {Area}" (the visible text starts the name), at least 48 px tall and 96 px wide.
- **Four reasons**, words only, no colour and no icon. A row that cannot be added shows its reason on line 3 (`--wl-ink-muted`) and keeps the Add button focusable with `aria-disabled="true"`, dimmed, `aria-describedby` pointing at the reason; a tap does nothing:
  - "Recovering" (the area was trained too recently, rule 7.2)
  - "Skipping today" (skipped on UF-08.1 or removed on this visit; **Add** is **not** disabled for an area removed on this visit: it puts it back. It is disabled only for an area skipped on UF-08.1, because that is a choice made one screen earlier)
  - "No exercise available" (every exercise for it is excluded or unavailable with your equipment or level)
  - "No time left" (it does not fit the minutes left)
  - The engine's `items_cap` (the workout already holds 8 exercises) reads "Workout is full" (D-0286).
- **Add** adds the area's top candidate at 3 sets, else 2 (`addArea`), puts the area in the title and the rows, and announces "{Area} added. {left} min left." Focus goes to the new row's **+** button (D-0275: the stepper is where the user continues).
- Over budget: every Add is refused with the over-budget line under the list, `role="status"` through the status line.

## "Fill the rest for me"

A `.wl-button--text` button (at least 44 × 44 px with padding, `--wl-ink` text, underline). It adds the engine's next available areas, one at a time (`areaCandidates`, first available, `addArea`), until none fits. It is **shown only while at least one area can still be added**, so with time left and an area available; and also at 0 areas (Start empty: shown). It is refused (stays shown, status line) while over budget. Announces "{n} areas added. {left} min left." (or "{Area} added. …" for one). **Focus:** after it runs, focus stays on the button; if it disappears (nothing more fits), focus moves to "Start workout".

## Time chips and rebuild

A chip rebuilds the plan for the new minutes. It keeps this visit's removed areas (skipped), swaps, adds and exclusions, and **drops the +/− set edits and added areas**; the status line says **"Plan rebuilt for {n} min. Your set changes were cleared."** (D-0274 §8). A main lift chosen on this visit stays; one that no longer fits is named in "Doesn't fit in {n} min: {names}." in the status line and more time brings it back. Focus stays on the chip.

## Focus mode disclosure (D-0280 §3)

- **Container:** a native `details` with the `summary` **"Focus mode"** (at least 48 px tall, chevron `aria-hidden`), **closed on every visit** (never remembered, whatever the last visit did), directly above "Start workout". `.wl-row` styling, no card. Opening it is not an edit and not a tap of the tap budget.
- **Content when open, in this DOM and focus order:**
  1. **"How focus mode works"** (`h2`, `.wl-label` role) and an ordered list of four steps, plain text (`--wl-ink`, no focus stops): "One thing on screen at a time." "Tap Done after each set." "Rest counts down by itself." "Everything else is behind pause."
  2. **Three switches**, each a `.wl-toggle` row on a native checkbox (row at least 48 px tall, box 24 px, between two 1 px `--wl-line` hairlines): **"Sound cues"**, **"Voice countdown 3-2-1"**, **"Keep screen awake"**. Same storage and defaults as UF-08.4 had: `localStorage["wl-focus-prefs"]`, all three on by default (D-0110 §6); a change is written at once and applies to the workout started next. The switches are device settings: they work offline and need no connection.
- Tab order inside the page: …, time chips, the "Focus mode" summary, then (only when open) the three switches in the order above, then "Start workout". A closed `details` has no focus stops inside it.
- When it opens, focus stays on the summary (the browser default). Escape does nothing special.
- No UF-09 control is added (principle 1): the disclosure is before Start and collapsed.

## "Start workout" (D-0280 §1, §2)

- `.wl-button--primary`, full width, at least 54 px tall, visible text **"Start workout"**, the only `--wl-action` fill on the screen (`--wl-on-action` on `--wl-action` 8.6:1). Written to the session exactly as UF-08.4's Start was: `clock()` read at the tap for `started_at`; one session id per visit to this step, made on the first tap, reused by a retry, dropped when the step unmounts; no network call, online or offline; one REPLACE navigation to `/session/<id>`, which opens UF-09.1 (D-0110 §1 to §5, D-0123). It writes the plan in the automatic order, as shown.
- **Normal:** enabled, with at least one area.
- **No areas** (every entry: Start empty, or a default plan where nothing fits, or everything removed): `aria-disabled="true"`, **still focusable**, `aria-describedby` pointing at the reason line **"Add at least one area."** (`--wl-ink-muted`, directly under the button, 5.9:1). A tap does nothing. Disabled look: `--wl-raise` fill with `--wl-ink` text, 6.2:1 (the primary button's disabled form, as UF-08.1).
- **Pending** (the session write is in flight): the button gets `aria-disabled="true"` and `aria-busy="true"`, the label stays "Start workout", and the status line says "Starting your workout." Every plan control (the rows, steppers, Add an area, Fill the rest, time chips, the Focus mode summary and switches) and the Back link and Close are **inert** (`aria-disabled` and ignored, T-0397), so the plan written is the plan shown. Nothing changes colour.
- **Failed** (the write was rejected): "Couldn't start the workout. Try again." (`role="alert"`, `--wl-ink`, directly under the button, replacing the reason line), the button and the plan controls are enabled again, focus returns to "Start workout", and a retry reuses the same session id. Offline is the same path (the write goes to the device first), so offline has no separate failure.
- **Interim (D-0280 §8):** until T-1009 lands, T-0996 and T-0997 build this screen with **"Looks good"** instead of "Start workout" and "Focus mode"; "Looks good" opens the built UF-08.4 Ready screen, which still shows the summary line, the four steps, the three switches and Start. That is a build step only. The spec on this page is the end state, and T-1009 replaces "Looks good" with "Start workout", removes UF-08.4 and moves its content here.

## Learning prompt card (T-1000, D-0277, D-0281)

Layer 1 of learn-from-edits (`docs/specs/learn-from-edits.md`). One card at most, above the area rows (Layout block 4), when a swap or removal count has reached its threshold. It asks once; it never writes without a tap on the yes button. **Absent from UF-08.1 and UF-09** (and UF-05.1): nothing is asked before the time question or during a workout (principle 1).

- **Region:** a wrapper `<div role="status">` (polite), **present on mount**, empty until the ledger is read; the card is inserted into it, so the title and line are announced. It has its own id; it is not the screen's status line (block 6).
- **Look (neutral notice pattern, `../components/neutral-notice.md`):** `--wl-raise` fill, 1 px `--wl-line` border, 12 px radius, 16 px padding, 2 px stroke info icon (`aria-hidden`). **Not** the attention treatment and not `--wl-action`. Only existing `--wl-*` variables, no raw colour, no new token.
- **Content, top to bottom:**
  1. Title (`h2`-level text, plan label role, `--wl-ink`): swap "Prefer {Dumbbell bench press} for {Chest}?" · removal "Stop suggesting {Cable crunch}?". `{Chest}` is the swap-in's first primary area.
  2. Line (`--wl-ink`, 15 px): swap "You've swapped {Bench press} for it {n} times." · removal "You've removed it {n} times." ({n} is the account's summed count, at least the threshold.)
  3. Buttons, in a row that wraps to a column at 320 px and at 200 % zoom, 8 px apart: the yes button as `.wl-button--secondary` ("Prefer it" or "Never suggest", at least 48 px tall) and **"No thanks"** as `.wl-button--text` (at least 44 px tall). **No `--wl-action` fill**: "Start workout" stays the one action fill on the screen.
  4. Offline only: the reason line (`--wl-ink`, 13 px, on `--wl-raise`) under the buttons.
- **Accessible names:** "Prefer it" and "Never suggest" are named with the exercise ("Prefer {Dumbbell bench press}", "Never suggest {Cable crunch}"; visible text starts the name); "No thanks" is named "No thanks, don't ask about {name} again".
- **No timeout, never a modal, never over another control.** It takes its place in the flow and pushes the rows down. Leaving the screen leaves the entry pending, so it shows again at the next mount. Only the answer makes it final ("asked" for good, D-0277).
- **Plan not rebuilt:** an answer changes the stored lists only; the plan shown stays as it is (preferences apply from the next plan).

## Status line and live region

One `role="status"` region, polite, **present on mount**, empty until used. It carries every change message (a region inserted already full is often missed):

- "{Area}: {k} sets, {left} min left." (after **+** or **−**) · "{Area} removed. Add it back below." · "{Area} added. {left} min left." · "{n} areas added. {left} min left." · "Plan rebuilt for {n} min. Your set changes were cleared." · "No areas right now. {n} are recovering or skipped today." · the refusal lines above · "Starting your workout."
- The bar line and the "Done by" line are **not** `aria-live`. The Start failure line is its own `role="alert"`.
- "Back" from UF-08.6 shows "{Area} removed. Add it back below." when that page took the last exercise out.

## Focus

- Tab order follows the layout: Close, Back, the first row's open button, its **−** (or Remove), **+**, the next row, …, "Add an area" summary (and, when open, its Add buttons), "Fill the rest for me", the five chips, the "Focus mode" summary (and, when open, the three switches), "Start workout".
- After **+** or **−** focus stays on the same button. After **Remove**, focus moves to the first stop of the row now in that place, or to the "Add an area" summary when no row is left. After Add area, focus goes to the new row's **+**. After a refusal, focus stays on the tapped button. Back from UF-08.6 lands on the open button of the row that was opened (UF-08.6.md).
- Focus never drops to `body`. Focus ring 2 px `--wl-focus`, offset 2 px, never removed.
