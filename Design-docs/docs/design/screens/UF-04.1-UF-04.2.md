# UF-04.1 Library and UF-04.2 Exercise detail: "Not suggested" and "Favorite"

- **Screens:** UF-04.1 Library (the tag) and UF-04.2 Exercise detail (the button and the state).
- **Decisions:** D-0199 (§10 design), D-0202 (§10 Favorite), D-0191 (toggle chip), D-0203 (foundation), D-0207 (body figure). Specs: `docs/specs/excluded-exercises.md`, `docs/specs/favorite-exercises.md`, `docs/specs/body-map-silhouette.md`.
- **Tokens:** `surface`, `surface-2`, `line`, `line-strong`, `text`, `text-muted`, `accent`, `on-accent`.

## UF-04.1 "Not suggested" tag

- A text tag on the library row of an excluded exercise: the words "Not suggested" (DM Sans 700, 11 px, uppercase, 0.08em tracking, `text-muted`) in a `surface-2` pill with a 1 px `line` border, after the name or on the second line when the name wraps.
- It is **text, never colour only**. The pill border and fill are not the signal; the words are. Screen readers read it as part of the row's name: "{name}, not suggested".
- The row is otherwise unchanged. The library shows excluded exercises as before (D-0199); history and balance are untouched.
- Offline: the tag shows (it reads the cache). It is not a control, so nothing is disabled.
- There is **no** such tag in UF-07.1 in v1.

## UF-04.2 Exercise detail

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Not excluded, online | **Don't suggest this**, a secondary button (`line-strong` border, `text`), full width, 48 px tall, placed below the main detail content. No confirm dialog |
| Excluded, online | The text "Not suggested" (same tag style as UF-04.1, larger 13 px) and **Suggest again**, a secondary button, 48 px tall |
| Offline (either state) | The button has `aria-disabled="true"` and `aria-describedby` pointing at one line under it: "Connect to change excluded exercises" (`text-muted`, 13 px). The state text still shows |
| Write pending | The button reads "Saving…", keeps its size, `aria-disabled="true"` |
| Write fails (online) | The state does not change, "Couldn't save. Try again." (`role="alert"`) shows under the button and the button is enabled again |

- After a successful tap: the tag and button swap, and a `role="status"` line (present on mount) says "{name} won't be suggested" or "{name} will be suggested again". **Focus stays on the button** (it moves to the other-state button in the same place).
- The button's accessible name includes the exercise: "Don't suggest {name}" and "Suggest again, {name}" (the visible text starts the name, WCAG 2.5.3).
- Past sets still count; the screen says nothing more.

## UF-04.1 "Favorite" tag (D-0202)

- A text tag on the library row of a favorite exercise: the word "Favorite" in the same slot and pill style as "Not suggested" (DM Sans 700, 11 px, uppercase, 0.08em tracking, `text-muted` on a `surface-2` pill with a 1 px `line` border), after the name or on the second line when the name wraps. A 12 px star icon (2 px stroke, `aria-hidden`) may precede the word; the word is the signal.
- Screen readers read "{name}, favorite".
- The two tags are mutually exclusive. If the caches ever disagree, only "Not suggested" shows (exclusion wins, D-0202 §3).
- At 320 px the tag wraps under the name; it never pushes the row's chevron or actions off screen.
- Offline: the tag shows (it reads the cache). Display only, never disabled.

## UF-04.2 "Favorite" toggle (D-0202)

- **Control:** a toggle chip (the D-0191 pattern: pill, 1 px `line-strong` border, `text`) labelled with a star icon (2 px stroke, `aria-hidden`) plus the word "Favorite". `aria-pressed` is `false` or `true`. Accessible name "Favorite {name}"; the visible word starts it (WCAG 2.5.3). At least 44 px tall and 44 px wide (in practice about 112 px).
- **Pressed:** `accent` fill, `on-accent` text, a filled star. **Unpressed:** `surface` fill, `line-strong` border, outlined star. The word and the filled/outlined star carry the state, not colour alone.
- **Placement:** in one action row below the main detail content (and below the D-0207 figure card, which is unchanged), with "Don't suggest this" (or "Suggest again" when excluded). At 390 px both sit side by side, each at least 48 px tall; "Favorite" is first, then "Don't suggest this" fills the remaining width. At 320 px, or with long names or 200 % text, they wrap to two full-width rows (Favorite first), without overlap and with an 8 px gap.
- **Status line:** one `role="status"` present on mount, under the action row, empty until used. After a tap it says nothing for a plain toggle (the pressed state is announced by the button). Move lines:
  - Favorite on an excluded exercise: "{name} is a favorite and will be suggested again."
  - "Don't suggest this" on a favorite: "{name} won't be suggested. Removed from favorites."
- **Mutual exclusion on screen:** an exercise that is excluded shows the "Not suggested" tag and "Suggest again", with the toggle unpressed. Tapping Favorite there removes the tag, shows "Don't suggest this" and presses the toggle. Tapping "Don't suggest this" on a favorite unpresses the toggle and shows the tag.
- **Focus** stays on the button tapped.

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Online | As above |
| Offline | The toggle has `aria-disabled="true"`, is dimmed (`text-muted`) and has `aria-describedby` pointing at the same single line as "Don't suggest this": "Connect to change favorites" is shown once under the action row, together with the excluded line when both apply (two lines at most, each its own target). Pressed state still shows (cache) |
| Write pending | The toggle keeps its size and `aria-pressed`, and has `aria-disabled="true"` |
| Write fails (online) | `aria-pressed` does not change, "Couldn't save. Try again." (`role="alert"`) shows under the action row, the toggle is enabled again |
| Zero history, 10 days away, time running out | No visual change |

- The accessible name "Favorite {name}" is unique on UF-04.2 (the screen has one exercise); on UF-11.6 the names are "Add …" and "Remove … from favorites", so the three forms never collide.
- Build: web-feature:UF-04 (T-0569), after T-0541.

## UF-04.2 Weight step (D-0251, D-0259)

- **Screen:** UF-04.2 Exercise detail. A "Weight step" row below the action row (Favorite / "Don't suggest this"). Spec: `docs/specs/rir-progression-and-increments.md`. Decisions: D-0251, D-0259 §7–§13, D-0211, D-0199, D-0202.
- **Look:** Cobalt, state `plan` (D-0208). Generic variables only: `--wl-ink`, `--wl-ink-muted`, `--wl-line`, `--wl-selected`, `--wl-on-selected`. No new token, no hex.
- **Hidden for timed and bodyweight:** the row is hidden (not disabled) for timed and bodyweight exercises (table below). It is **editable offline** because the write is queued (table below).
- **Meaning:** how many kg the suggested weight goes up each time. It is the exercise's own step, or the library default when the user has not set one.

### Layout

1. Label "Weight step" (label role, `--wl-ink-muted`) and, on the same row or wrapped under it, the visible current value: "Weight step: 1.25 kg (yours)" when an override exists, "Weight step: 2.5 kg (default)" otherwise.
2. Helper line "How much the weight goes up each time." (`--wl-ink-muted`, 13 px), tied to the group with `aria-describedby`.
3. The chip group, `role="radiogroup"` labelled "Weight step": four chips **1.25**, **2.5**, **5**, **Other**, in that order. Pill radius, 1 px `--wl-line` border, `--wl-ink` text, at least 44 px tall and 44 px wide, 8 px gap, wrapping at 320 px or 200 % text.
4. Only while an override exists: a text button "Use default ({n} kg)" ({n} the library default, through `Intl`). At least 44 px tall.
5. The "Other" field (only when Other is open, below).
6. One `role="status"` line, present on mount and empty until used, and one `role="alert"` line for a failed save.

### Chips

- It is a radiogroup, not `aria-pressed` buttons: exactly one chip is selected. Each chip is `role="radio"` with `aria-checked`. Uses a roving tabindex: the selected chip (or the first when none is) has `tabindex="0"`, the others `-1`. Follows the WAI radio-group pattern: Arrow keys (and Home and End) move focus **and select**, Tab leaves the group, Space or Enter on a focused chip selects it. The write is **debounced 600 ms after the last selection change**, so arrowing across chips saves only the final value, with one status announcement for it. Space or Enter on a chip commits at once (no wait). Arrowing onto **Other** selects it (`aria-checked="true"`) and opens the field **without moving focus**; Tab then moves into the field. Arrowing onto Other writes nothing until Save.
- **Selected:** `--wl-selected` fill, `--wl-on-selected` text and a check mark (2 px stroke, `aria-hidden`) before the number. Selection is the fill plus the check mark, never colour alone. **Unselected:** transparent with the `--wl-line` border and `--wl-ink` text.
- Accessible names start with the visible text (WCAG 2.5.3). The visible chip text is the bare number ("1.25", "2.5", "5") and the accessible name adds the unit ("1.25 kg", "2.5 kg", "5 kg"); the Other chip is "Other".
- Numbers are formatted with `Intl.NumberFormat` (the user's locale decimal mark); the copy below shows English.
- **The chip equal to the library default is a reset.** Choosing it deletes the override (the row goes back to "(default)"). It is selected whenever there is no override. When the default is not 1.25, 2.5 or 5 (for example 1) and there is no override, **no chip is checked** and the first chip has `tabindex="0"` (the group stays reachable by Tab). No list chip is the default, so the "Use default ({n} kg)" button is the way back.
- **Use default ({n} kg):** a text button shown only while an override exists. It does the same as choosing the default chip. Focus stays on the button's place (moves to the selected chip when the button leaves the DOM).

### Other

- Choosing **Other** opens a field under the chips: label "Step in kg" (visible), `inputmode="decimal"`, a **Save** button and a **Cancel** button, each at least 44 px.
- Comma or dot is accepted as the decimal mark. Valid: a multiple of 0.25 from 0.25 to 10 inclusive.
- Validate on **Save** and on blur, **except when blur goes to Cancel** (no error then). An empty value on Save is an error too. On error the field gets `aria-invalid="true"` and `aria-describedby` pointing at the error: "Enter a step from 0.25 to 10 kg, in multiples of 0.25." (`--wl-ink`, with an icon, never colour alone). The error clears when the value is valid. When the alert shows, the status line is cleared.
- **Cancel and Escape** close the field and restore the previous selection and value (no error, no write). Focus returns to the Other chip.
- An off-list saved value (for example 1.5) keeps Other selected and shows the chip as "Other: 1.5 kg". The visible value line reads "Weight step: 1.5 kg (yours)".
- A valid value equal to the library default is a reset, like the default chip.

### Feedback

- One `role="status"` line, present on mount. Templates: "Weight step set to {n} kg." after setting a value (for example "Weight step set to 1.25 kg."), "Weight step back to the default, {n} kg." after a reset (for example "Weight step back to the default, 2.5 kg."). {n} goes through `Intl`. The next change replaces it. When the `role="alert"` line shows, the status line is cleared, so the two never speak together.
- A failed save: the row reverts to the previous value, and a `role="alert"` line "Couldn't save. Try again." shows under the row. There is no undo.
- Focus stays on the chip or button used.

### When the row shows

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Barbell, dumbbell, machine, cable (external load) | The row |
| Timed or bodyweight exercise | The row is hidden (not disabled), not in the DOM, and leaves no gap. One shared external-load predicate decides this for the UI and the engine |
| Online | As above |
| Offline | The row is editable offline. The device store for the step and its queued write are **new work from T-0954**; today's Favorite and "Don't suggest this" are online-only, so there is no existing pattern to copy. A change applies at once and syncs later, last write wins by `updated_at`. This is a stated carve-out from D-0199 and D-0202, which disable UF-04.2 controls offline ("Connect to change...") only for writes that have no queue. No "Connect to change..." copy on this row, no `aria-disabled`. The other offline-disabled controls stay as they are |
| Save pending | The chips stay enabled. The change shows at once (queued write) |
| Save fails | Reverts, the alert above |

### Accessibility

- The selected chip: `--wl-on-selected` on `--wl-selected` is 8.6:1 (needs 4.5). Unselected text `--wl-ink` on the screen background is 8.6:1; `--wl-ink-muted` helper text is 5.9:1. The chip border is decorative, because the number and the check mark identify each chip.
- Targets at least 44 px. Focus ring 2 px, offset 2 px. Forced colours: selected chip `Highlight` fill with `HighlightText`, the check mark stays.
- Build: web-feature:UF-04 (T-0956, after T-0954 and this spec).

## Accessibility (D-0199 §10)

- The tag is text, never colour only. Contrast: `text-muted` on `surface-2` 5.8:1 (needs 4.5).
- Offline: `aria-disabled` plus one `aria-describedby` per screen. "Couldn't save. Try again." is `role="alert"`.
- Targets at least 44 px. Focus ring 2 px `accent`, offset 2 px.
- No UF-09 control is added.

## UF-04.2 Body figure

- **Screen:** UF-04.2 Exercise detail. Only the figure and the area lists change. The header, how-to, mistakes, variations, attribution, My history, Favorite and "Don't suggest this" are unchanged.
- **Decision:** D-0207 §3. Spec: `docs/specs/body-map-silhouette.md` (§3 asset, §5 sub-muscles, §7 accessibility, AC-4/5/11).
- **Component:** `BodyFigure` (shared, `components/body-figure`), built from `../assets/body-figure/body-figure.svg`. Tokens: `surface`, `surface-2`, `line`, `line-strong`, `text`, `text-muted`, `accent`, `on-accent`. Stroke and hatch rules: `design-system.md` § Body figure.

### Layout (390 × 844 frame, 20 px gutter)

```
Back Squat                         ← h1 (unchanged)
Compound · Squat pattern · Barbell ← tagline (unchanged)
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│     FRONT            BACK        │  figure card: surface, 1 px line, radius 16, padding 16
│    (figure)        (figure)      │  figure 200 px tall, centred, both views always shown
│                                  │
│ PRIMARY ■                        │  card label (12 px, 700, uppercase, text-muted) + swatch
│ [Quads] [Glutes]                 │  existing pills (D-0079 §2)
│ SECONDARY ▨                      │
│ [Hamstrings] [Core]              │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
How to …                           ← unchanged from here down
```

- The figure card replaces the two bare pill lists at the same spot (under the tagline). The lists move **into** the card, under the figure.
- The figure is centred, 200 px tall, and its width follows the viewBox (about 177 px). The card has no title. The labels name what the fills mean.

### Fills

| Area weight (`exercise.areas[a]`) | Region style | Swatch in the list label |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 (primary) | Solid `accent` | 12 × 12 px solid `accent`, radius 3 |
| 0.5 (secondary) | `accent` diagonal hatch (`wl-fig-hatch`), 1.5 px stripes at 45°, 5 px period, on `surface-2` | 12 × 12 px, the same hatch, 1 px `text-muted` border |
| absent | `surface-2` | — |

- Every region has a 1 px `text-muted` border. The body outline is 1.5 px `text-muted`. Seams inside `accent` regions use `on-accent`, and elsewhere `line-strong` (decorative).
- An area that appears in both views (shoulders, arms) is filled in both.
- The fills come only from `exercise.areas`. Nothing is computed beyond reading 1.0 or 0.5.
- Regions here aren't tappable (D-0207 §3).

### List labels

- Above each pill list there's a visible label: **"Primary"** and **"Secondary"** (card label style), followed by the swatch. The swatch is `aria-hidden`.
- The lists keep their accessible names: "Primary areas" and "Secondary areas" (`en.uf04.primaryAreas` / `secondaryAreas`). The visible word starts the name (WCAG 2.5.3).
- No secondary areas: the Secondary label and list are not rendered. The hatch is then not on screen either, so no legend for it is needed.
- The pills are unchanged: text, area names in the fixed order (engine rule 1).
- New strings (`apps/web/src/lib/i18n/flows/uf-04.ts`): `primaryLabel: "Primary"` and `secondaryLabel: "Secondary"`. No other copy. No sub-muscle names in v1.

### Figure states

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Loading (no data yet) | Unchanged: the empty `wl-uf04` container. The figure renders only with data. |
| Exercise with areas | As above. |
| Exercise with no area weights (data error) | No figure card and no lists. The rest of the screen as today. |
| Detail text missing (`detail === null`) | The figure card still shows (it needs only the library row), then "detailMissing". |
| Offline | Identical. The figure is bundled inline with no network. |
| Excluded / Favorite | No effect on the figure. |

### Figure accessibility

- The `<svg>` is `aria-hidden="true"` and not focusable. The text equivalent is the two named lists directly under it.
- Primary and secondary differ by **pattern** (solid versus hatched) as well as by the list text. Contrast: `accent` against `surface` is 13.5:1, against `surface-2` 11.5:1, and the hatch stripes against their ground 11.5:1. The region border `text-muted` is 6.8:1 on `surface`.
- No new controls, so no new targets. The tab order is unchanged.
- Forced colours: primary `CanvasText` fill, secondary `CanvasText` hatch on `Canvas`, borders `CanvasText`.
- Text zoom 200 %: the pills wrap. The figure keeps its size.
- Build: web-feature:UF-04, after the `BodyFigure` component (T-0556).

## UF-04.2 One side at a time (D-0261, D-0263 §7)

- **Screen:** UF-04.2 Exercise detail, flagged exercises only (`unilateral` is `arm`, `leg` or `side`). Spec: `docs/specs/unilateral-exercises.md`. No new token (D-0208).
- **Where:** one static body-text `<p>` directly under the steps. It has no role, no `aria-live` and no control. It uses the same type and `--wl-ink` colour as the step text, so contrast is as the steps.
- **Copy, non-timed** (arm, leg or side as the row says): "Do all reps on one arm, then switch. Log reps for one arm. One set covers both arms. Log RIR for your weaker side." For a leg row "arm" reads "leg", and for a side row "side" (and "both sides").
- **Copy, timed** (side-plank): "Hold one side, then switch. Log seconds for one side." There is no RIR sentence on a timed row.
- A bilateral row has no extra line and no gap: the screen is as today.
- **Offline:** the flag comes from the cached library, so the line reads the same. A missing or null flag shows no line.
- **320 px:** the line wraps and never truncates.
- Strings come from the shared module (`perSide`, see `UF-09.3-UF-09.4.md`). Build: `web-feature:UF-04`.
