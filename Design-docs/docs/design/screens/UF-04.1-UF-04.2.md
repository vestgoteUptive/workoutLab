# UF-04.1 Library and UF-04.2 Exercise detail: "Not suggested"

- **Screens:** UF-04.1 Library (the tag) and UF-04.2 Exercise detail (the button and the state).
- **Decisions:** D-0199 (§10 design), D-0207 (body figure). Specs: `docs/specs/excluded-exercises.md`, `docs/specs/body-map-silhouette.md`.
- **Tokens:** `surface`, `surface-2`, `line`, `line-strong`, `text`, `text-muted`, `accent`, `on-accent`.

## UF-04.1 "Not suggested" tag

- A text tag on the library row of an excluded exercise: the words "Not suggested" (DM Sans 700, 11 px, uppercase, 0.08em tracking, `text-muted`) in a `surface-2` pill with a 1 px `line` border, after the name or on the second line when the name wraps.
- It is **text, never colour only**. The pill border and fill are not the signal; the words are. Screen readers read it as part of the row's name: "{name}, not suggested".
- The row is otherwise unchanged. The library shows excluded exercises as before (D-0199); history and balance are untouched.
- Offline: the tag shows (it reads the cache). It is not a control, so nothing is disabled.
- There is **no** such tag in UF-07.1 in v1.

## UF-04.2 Exercise detail

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Not excluded, online | **Don't suggest this**, a secondary button (`line-strong` border, `text`), full width, 48 px tall, placed below the main detail content. No confirm dialog |
| Excluded, online | The text "Not suggested" (same tag style as UF-04.1, larger 13 px) and **Suggest again**, a secondary button, 48 px tall |
| Offline (either state) | The button has `aria-disabled="true"` and `aria-describedby` pointing at one line under it: "Connect to change excluded exercises" (`text-muted`, 13 px). The state text still shows |
| Write pending | The button reads "Saving…", keeps its size, `aria-disabled="true"` |
| Write fails (online) | The state does not change, "Couldn't save. Try again." (`role="alert"`) shows under the button and the button is enabled again |

- After a successful tap: the tag and button swap, and a `role="status"` line (present on mount) says "{name} won't be suggested" or "{name} will be suggested again". **Focus stays on the button** (it moves to the other-state button in the same place).
- The button's accessible name includes the exercise: "Don't suggest {name}" and "Suggest again, {name}" (the visible text starts the name, WCAG 2.5.3).
- Past sets still count; the screen says nothing more.

## Accessibility (D-0199 §10)

- The tag is text, never colour only. Contrast: `text-muted` on `surface-2` 5.8:1 (needs 4.5).
- Offline: `aria-disabled` plus one `aria-describedby` per screen. "Couldn't save. Try again." is `role="alert"`.
- Targets at least 44 px. Focus ring 2 px `accent`, offset 2 px.
- No UF-09 control is added.

## UF-04.2 Body figure

- **Screen:** UF-04.2 Exercise detail. Only the figure and the area lists change. The header, how-to, mistakes, variations, attribution, My history, Favorite and "Don't suggest this" are unchanged.
- **Decision:** D-0207 §3. Spec: `docs/specs/body-map-silhouette.md` (§3 asset, §5 sub-muscles, §7 accessibility, AC-4/5/11).
- **Component:** `BodyFigure` (shared, `components/body-figure`), built from `../assets/body-figure/body-figure.svg`. Tokens: `surface`, `surface-2`, `line`, `line-strong`, `text`, `text-muted`, `accent`, `on-accent`. Stroke and hatch rules: `design-system.md` § Body figure.

### Layout (390 × 844 frame, 20 px gutter)

```

┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│     FRONT            BACK        │  figure card: surface, 1 px line, radius 16, padding 16
│    (figure)        (figure)      │  figure 200 px tall, centred, both views always shown
│                                  │
│ PRIMARY ■                        │  card label (12 px, 700, uppercase, text-muted) + swatch

│ SECONDARY ▨                      │
│ [Hamstrings] [Core]              │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
How to …                           ← unchanged from here down
```

- The figure card replaces the two bare pill lists at the same spot (under the tagline). The lists move **into** the card, under the figure.
- The figure is centred, 200 px tall, and its width follows the viewBox (about 177 px). The card has no title. The labels name what the fills mean.

### Fills

| Area weight (`exercise.areas[a]`) | Region style | Swatch in the list label |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 (primary) | Solid `accent` | 12 × 12 px solid `accent`, radius 3 |
| 0.5 (secondary) | `accent` diagonal hatch (`wl-fig-hatch`), 1.5 px stripes at 45°, 5 px period, on `surface-2` | 12 × 12 px, the same hatch, 1 px `text-muted` border |
| absent | `surface-2` | — |

- Every region has a 1 px `text-muted` border. The body outline is 1.5 px `text-muted`. Seams inside `accent` regions use `on-accent`, and elsewhere `line-strong` (decorative).
- An area that appears in both views (shoulders, arms) is filled in both.
- The fills come only from `exercise.areas`. Nothing is computed beyond reading 1.0 or 0.5.
- Regions here aren't tappable (D-0207 §3).

### List labels

- Above each pill list there's a visible label: **"Primary"** and **"Secondary"** (card label style), followed by the swatch. The swatch is `aria-hidden`.
- The lists keep their accessible names: "Primary areas" and "Secondary areas" (`en.uf04.primaryAreas` / `secondaryAreas`). The visible word starts the name (WCAG 2.5.3).
- No secondary areas: the Secondary label and list are not rendered. The hatch is then not on screen either, so no legend for it is needed.
- The pills are unchanged: text, area names in the fixed order (engine rule 1).
- New strings (`apps/web/src/lib/i18n/flows/uf-04.ts`): `primaryLabel: "Primary"` and `secondaryLabel: "Secondary"`. No other copy. No sub-muscle names in v1.

### Figure states

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Loading (no data yet) | Unchanged: the empty `wl-uf04` container. The figure renders only with data. |
| Exercise with areas | As above. |
| Exercise with no area weights (data error) | No figure card and no lists. The rest of the screen as today. |
| Detail text missing (`detail === null`) | The figure card still shows (it needs only the library row), then "detailMissing". |
| Offline | Identical. The figure is bundled inline with no network. |
| Excluded / Favorite | No effect on the figure. |

### Figure accessibility

- The `<svg>` is `aria-hidden="true"` and not focusable. The text equivalent is the two named lists directly under it.
- Primary and secondary differ by **pattern** (solid versus hatched) as well as by the list text. Contrast: `accent` against `surface` is 13.5:1, against `surface-2` 11.5:1, and the hatch stripes against their ground 11.5:1. The region border `text-muted` is 6.8:1 on `surface`.
- No new controls, so no new targets. The tab order is unchanged.
- Forced colours: primary `CanvasText` fill, secondary `CanvasText` hatch on `Canvas`, borders `CanvasText`.
- Text zoom 200 %: the pills wrap. The figure keeps its size.
- Build: web-feature:UF-04, after the `BodyFigure` component (T-0556).
