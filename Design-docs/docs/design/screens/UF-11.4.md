# UF-11.4 Account settings

- **Screen:** UF-11.4 Account settings (`/plan/account`, no tab bar, reached only from the UF-11.2 header link "Account and sign out"). This is the GitHub #45 visual rework. Behaviour is unchanged.
- **Decisions:**
- **Look:** Cobalt + state colour (D-0208); state `plan`; patterns in `components/state-patterns.md`. Structure comes from `.wl-section` blocks divided by a 1 px `--wl-line` hairline above each, with no card fill (D-0208 §3).
  - D-0136: export, delete, typed confirm, the wipe, all the copy;
  - D-0195: the order, Sign out under the email, local scope, the unsynced confirm;
  - D-0168 §6: Equipment is a section here;
  - D-0199 / D-0200: the export includes excluded exercises (data only, no UI change here).
- **Foundation:** `docs/specs/visual-foundation.md` (fonts, `.wl-page`, type scale, `.wl-section`, buttons, text input). That spec ships first.
- **Components:** C-03 Checkbox (`components/c-03-checkbox.md`) for the equipment list, and the neutral notice (`components/neutral-notice.md`) for the unsynced message. Both already exist.
- **Tokens:** existing only: `--wl-bg`, `--wl-raise`, `--wl-raise`, `--wl-line`, `--wl-ink-muted`, `--wl-ink`, `--wl-ink-muted`, `--wl-action`, `--wl-on-action`. No danger colour. See "Destructive action" and the open decision in the handback.

## What was wrong (#45)

On prod the screen is one column of loose paragraphs and same-looking 8 px-radius buttons in the system font, flush against the screen edge:

- the email line, a "Sign out" button with no heading;
- a bare `<fieldset>` with nine accent-filled chips;
- "Your data" and "Delete account" as browser-default `h2`s.

Nothing groups or ranks the actions, the destructive action looks like every other button, and there is **no way back to Plan** in the installed PWA. The route hides the tab bar, and iOS standalone mode has no browser back button.

## Layout (top to bottom)

The screen root is `.wl-page` (20 px gutter, 640 px max width, 16 px between blocks).

### 1. Back link
- A text button at the top left: a 2 px stroke chevron-left icon plus "Plan", → `/plan`. Min 44 × 44 px, DM Sans 500 16 px, `--wl-ink`. Accessible name "Back to Plan".
- New copy: "Plan" (reuse `en.screens.plan`) with the accessible name "Back to Plan".
- **Why it's needed:** the route has no tab bar (D-0136 §1). Without this link, an installed iOS PWA user is stuck on the screen unless they sign out.

### 2. Title
- `<h1>` "Account settings" (page title style).

### 3. Account card (D-0195: email and Sign out together, first)
- `.wl-section`. Label-style `h2`: "Signed in as".
- The email: body 16 px, weight 500, `--wl-ink`, `overflow-wrap: anywhere` (a long address wraps and is never truncated). When the stored email isn't available (`storedEmail` returns null), the label reads "Account" and there is no email line.
- **Sign out:** the secondary button, full card width, min height 48 px, with a 2 px stroke sign-out icon (`aria-hidden`). While working it reads "Signing out…" and is disabled. It is enabled offline (D-0195 §5).
- **Unsynced confirm** (D-0195 §4), shown in place of the button as the paper panel (`components/state-patterns.md`, full-bleed `paper.bg`, `paper.ink` text, `--wl-focus` = `paper.ink`):
  - the neutral notice with "Some workouts haven't synced yet. They stay on this device and upload the next time you sign in here.";
  - then "Sign out anyway" (the secondary button, full width), which gets focus when the panel opens;
  - then "Cancel" (a text button, 44 px). Cancel returns focus to Sign out.

### 4. Equipment card (D-0168 §6)
- `.wl-section` wrapping the existing `<fieldset>`. The `<legend>` is in label style: "Your equipment". The hint follows in secondary style: "We only suggest exercises you can do with what you tick."
- **The nine items are C-03 checkboxes, not accent-filled chips.**
  - They sit in a grid `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(140px, 1fr))` with a 4 px row gap: two columns at 360–390 px, more on wider screens.
  - Each row is at least 44 px, label text DM Sans 500 15 px.
  - Checked shows the C-03 `--wl-action` box with an `--wl-on-action` tick, so the state is never shown by fill colour alone. Today's chips use only a whole-chip fill.
- **Save:** the primary button (full width, 54 px), the one accent action on this screen. While nothing has changed it is disabled (`--wl-raise` fill, `--wl-ink-muted` text), as today.
  - "Saving" while the write runs.
  - Then "Saved" as a `role="status"` caption under the button.
  - On failure, "Couldn't save your equipment. Try again." as `role="alert"`.
- Offline: Save is disabled, with the caption "Connect to save" under it.
- Cold cache: the card shows only the legend and "Your equipment isn't on this device yet." (caption, `--wl-ink-muted`).

### 5. Your data card
- `.wl-section`. Label-style `h2`: "Your data". The body follows in secondary style: "Download everything we store for you as one JSON file."
- **Export my data:** the secondary button, full width, 48 px, with a 2 px stroke download icon. While working it reads "Preparing your file…" and is disabled.
- Offline: the button is disabled, with the caption "Connect to export your data." under it.
- Failure: "Couldn't export your data. Try again." with `role="alert"`, under the button.

### 6. Delete account card (last, set apart)
- 32 px above it instead of 16, so it is visually separated from the routine settings.
- `.wl-section` with a 1 px **`--wl-ink-muted`** top rule instead of `--wl-line`. It is the one section with the stronger edge.
- Label-style `h2`: "Delete account".
- **Closed:** a one-line caption, then the button.
  - The caption (secondary, `--wl-ink-muted`) is new copy: "Permanently removes your account and all your data."
  - The button is "Delete account…", secondary, full width, 48 px.
- **Open** (the D-0136 §3 inline panel):
  - the permanence text (body 15 px, `--wl-ink`): "This deletes your account and every workout, set, routine and plan, on our servers and on this device. It can't be undone. Export your data first if you want a copy.";
  - a visible `<label>` "Type delete to confirm" (caption, 13 px, `--wl-ink-muted`) above the text input;
  - the text input (foundation style: `--wl-bg` fill, 1 px `--wl-ink-muted` border, 48 px, radius 12 px), which gets focus when the panel opens;
  - "Delete my account" (secondary, full width), enabled only when the typed word matches. Disabled, it uses `--wl-raise` fill and `--wl-ink-muted` text. While working it reads "Deleting…";
  - "Cancel" (a text button). Cancel returns focus to "Delete account…".
- Offline: "Delete account…" is disabled, with the caption "Connect to delete your account."
- Errors use `role="alert"` under the panel: "Your sign-in has expired. Sign in again, then delete your account." and "Couldn't delete your account. Try again."

### Destructive action
- There is no danger token. The design system's orange is reserved for PRs, warm-up, rest and over-time, and the C-01 outline. Delete gets its seriousness from:
  - its **position** (last, set apart);
  - the **stronger card edge**;
  - the **explicit copy**;
  - the **typed confirm**.
- It is never the accent button. Accent means "do this next", and on this screen that is Save.

## Order (D-0195 §1, top to bottom)

Back link → title → Signed in as + Sign out → Equipment → Your data → Delete account. No other order is allowed.

## States

| State | What changes |
|---|---|
| Online, nothing pending | Every action is enabled except Save (until a change) and Delete my account (until typed). |
| Offline | Sign out stays enabled. Save, Export my data and Delete account… are disabled, each with its own "Connect to …" caption directly under its button. |
| No user id yet | Sign out, Export and Delete are disabled (as today). |
| Signing out | "Signing out…", the button disabled, the rest unchanged until navigation to `/welcome`. |
| Unsynced work on Sign out | The paper panel (neutral notice text + "Sign out anyway" / "Cancel") replaces the button inside the account section. |
| Exporting / export failed | "Preparing your file…" / the `role="alert"` line. |
| Delete panel open / deleting / failed | As in block 6. |
| Long email (60+ characters) | Wraps inside the card. No horizontal scroll at 320 px. |

## Copy

All existing (D-0136 §8, D-0195 §6, T-0216) except:

- new: "Permanently removes your account and all your data." (the closed-state caption on the delete card);
- new: the accessible name "Back to Plan" (the visible text reuses "Plan");
- new: "Account" (the card label when there is no email);
- changed use: "Signed in as {email}" becomes a label "Signed in as" plus the email on its own line. The `signedInAs(email)` string can stay as the card's accessible description.

## Accessibility

- Heading outline: `h1` Account settings, then the `h2`s "Signed in as" (or "Account"), "Your data" and "Delete account". Equipment uses its `legend`.
- Each disabled action is explained by visible text right under it, linked with `aria-describedby`.
- Contrast:
  - the text input and C-03 boundaries are `--wl-ink-muted`: 5.9:1 on `plan.bg` (needs 3);
  - `--wl-ink-muted` on `plan.bg` is 5.9:1; `--wl-ink` is 8.6:1;
  - `--wl-on-action` on `--wl-action` is 8.6:1 (`plan.on-action` on white).
- Targets: at least 44 px everywhere. Primary buttons are 54 px, secondary buttons 48 px, and text buttons and checkbox rows 44 px.
- Focus ring: 2 px `--wl-action` at a 2 px offset. Focus moves into an opened panel and back to its opener on Cancel (as today).
- Never reachable from UF-03, UF-08 or UF-09.

## Acceptance criteria (web-feature:UF-11 build ticket)

1. The DOM order of `h1`, the account card, the equipment `fieldset`, Your data and Delete account matches D-0195 §1. The Sign out button is inside the same card as the email (vitest).
2. A "Back to Plan" link to `/plan` is the first focusable element (vitest; Playwright: tapping it lands on UF-11.2).
3. The equipment items are C-03 checkboxes (`input[type=checkbox]` inside `label`, 22 px box). Toggling one enables Save. Exactly one primary-class button is on the screen (vitest).
4. Offline: Sign out is enabled. Save, Export and Delete account… are disabled, each with `aria-describedby` pointing at its visible "Connect to …" caption (vitest).
5. The delete card has the `--wl-ink-muted` border class and comes last. "Delete my account" is never the primary class (vitest).
6. Playwright at 390 × 844: visual-foundation T-1 and G-2 hold. At 320 × 640, with a 64-character email, there is no horizontal scroll and the email isn't clipped.
7. Playwright: every interactive element is at least 44 px tall. The text input's border colour equals `--wl-ink-muted`.
8. Every existing UF-11.4 behaviour test (export, delete, sign-out, unsynced confirm, focus return) still passes unchanged, apart from selectors that name the replaced chip class.
