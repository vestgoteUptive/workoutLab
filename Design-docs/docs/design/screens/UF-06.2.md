## Est. 1RM card and delta line

- The card label is "Est. 1RM". The value is "about {N} kg", N the nearest whole kg (`formatWeight`), never a decimal and never without "about". Example: "about 124 kg".
- The delta line is a **plain paragraph** (not a live region), in `--wl-ink-muted`, under the cards. It compares the **latest point with the first point in the window**, in rounded kg. Words carry the direction, not colour or an arrow:
  - up: "Up 3 kg since 12 Oct"
  - down: "Down 2 kg since 12 Oct"
  - equal after rounding: "Same as 12 Oct"
- **Delta = round(last) − round(first)**, each rounded to the nearest whole kg first, so it always equals the difference of the two numbers a reader sees (AC6: 118 to 124 gives "Up 6 kg"). Zero difference reads "Same as {date}".
- The date is "d MMM". With one point there is no delta line.

### y axis algorithm

1. `lo` = the largest multiple of **5 kg strictly below** the lowest point; `hi` = the smallest multiple of 5 kg **strictly above** the highest (values unrounded).
2. If `hi − lo < 10`: `lo −= 5` and `hi += 5`. This is the degenerate case: one point, or all points equal, gives ±5 kg around the value (100 kg gives 95 to 105).
3. If `hi − lo > 20` and `(hi − lo) / 5` is odd: `hi += 5`, so the middle gridline falls on a multiple of 5.
4. **2 gridlines** (at `lo` and `hi`) when `hi − lo ≤ 20`; **3** (at `lo`, the middle and `hi`) when it is greater than 20.
5. y maps linearly from `lo` (at y = 120) to `hi` (at y = 28).

### One point

- The dot sits at the **centre** of the plot area (between the left and right reserves). No line.
- The first and last date are the same, so the date is shown **once**, `text-anchor: middle` under the dot on the date-row baseline.
- The note "One session so far. Log it again to see a trend." sits **directly under the chart, above the table toggle**. Order: chart, note, toggle, explanation.

- **Same-day sessions:** two sessions on one local date keep both points at the **same x**, drawn as a **vertical step**. The table has one row for each (D-0258 §5).
- **Never colour only:** the latest point has its text label, the card repeats the value, and the delta line says the direction in words.
- **Contrast line.** Gridlines are decorative, `--wl-line` on `--wl-bg` is 2.1:1 (D-0211 §4). All chart text, `--wl-ink-muted` on `--wl-bg`, is at least 4.5:1. The line and the dots, `--wl-action` on `--wl-bg`, are at least 3:1. The design-tokens test computes these from `tokens.json`.

## Accessibility

- The SVG has `role="img"` and an `aria-label` summary (count, first and last value and date):
  - two or more points: "Estimated one-rep max, {n} sessions, from about {first} kg on {d} to about {last} kg on {d}.", for example "Estimated one-rep max, 5 sessions, from about 118 kg on 12 Oct to about 124 kg on 18 Nov."
  - one point: "Estimated one-rep max, 1 session, about {value} kg on {d}."
  - {first}, {last} and {value} are whole kg, as in the card.
- Every `<text>` inside the SVG is `aria-hidden` (`aria-hidden="true"`) so it is not read twice. The delta line is a plain paragraph, not a live region.
- Touch targets at least 44 px. Focus ring is the generic `--wl-focus`.
- Zoom to 200 %: card text and table wrap, the chart scales.

## Table alternative

- **Toggle:** a `<button>` directly under the chart, at least **44 px** tall, labelled "Show as table" and, when open, "Hide table". It has `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` pointing at the table's container. **The container (a `<div>` with a stable `id`) is always rendered and is empty while collapsed**, so `aria-controls` never points at a missing element.
- **Table:** a real `<table>`, rendered **only when expanded**, caption "Estimated 1RM per session". Columns: **Date** (each cell is a row header, `scope="row"`), **Best set** ("100 kg × 8", the multiplication sign, **reuse `weightedBest`** from `lib/i18n/flows/uf-06.ts`), **Est. 1RM** ("about 127 kg"). One row per point, **oldest first**. Dates "d MMM".
- The chart stays visible when the table is open. **Focus stays on the toggle** when it opens and closes. No animation.
