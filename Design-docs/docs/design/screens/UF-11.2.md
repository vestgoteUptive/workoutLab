# UF-11.2 Plan

- **Screen:** UF-11.2 Plan (`/plan`, the Plan tab). This spec covers the whole screen: the GitHub #37 rework, plus the Excluded exercises row from D-0199, which is kept as written below.
- **Decisions:** D-0202 (Favorite exercises row), D-0002 (user flows v2), D-0018 / D-0070 (check-in), D-0081 ("First" vs "Next check-in"), D-0195 (header link "Account and sign out"; the check-in card is the first element after the header), D-0199 §10 (Excluded exercises row; links to `UF-11.5.md`).
- **Look:** Cobalt + state colour (D-0208); state `plan`; patterns in `components/state-patterns.md`. Structure comes from `.wl-section` blocks divided by a 1 px `--wl-line` hairline above each, with no card fill (D-0208 §3).
- **Foundation:** `docs/specs/visual-foundation.md` (fonts, `.wl-page` gutter, type scale, `.wl-section`, `.wl-row`, `.wl-label`, buttons). That spec ships first.
- **Tokens:** existing only: `--wl-bg`, `--wl-raise`, `--wl-raise`, `--wl-line`, `--wl-ink-muted`, `--wl-ink`, `--wl-ink-muted`, `--wl-action`, `--wl-on-action`. No new token and no new component.

## What this screen is for (answer to #37)

Plan answers one question: **"What am I aiming for?"** It shows the goal, how often you train, and the hard sets each body area aims for over 14 days. It also shows how the plan changes itself (check-ins, principle 4) and is the one place to change it (Edit plan).

It is **not** where you see progress: that is Balance (UF-10.1, "Where am I this period?"). The prod screen mixed the two jobs into nine identical text rows with nothing to act on. The rework groups the content into cards that each answer one question, shows the targets as compact numbers, and gives the screen one clear primary action.

## Layout (top to bottom)

The screen root is `.wl-page` (20 px gutter, 640 px max width, 16 px between blocks).

### 1. Header (`.wl-plan__header`, a `<header>`)
- One row: `<h1>` "Plan" (page title style: Big Shoulders Display 800, uppercase) on the left. On the right, the D-0195 link "Account and sign out" → `/plan/account`.
  - The link is a compact secondary button: `--wl-raise` fill, 1 px `--wl-ink-muted` border, radius 14 px, min height 44 px, DM Sans 500 14 px, `--wl-ink`.
  - It has a 2 px stroke person icon (18 px, `aria-hidden`) before the text.
  - At narrow widths the row wraps, and the link drops under the title, left-aligned.
- Under the row, a **purpose line** `<p>` (secondary style, 14 px, `--wl-ink-muted`), max 2 lines at 390 px:
  "Your goal, rhythm and the hard sets each area aims for every 14 days. It adapts to what you actually do."
- The purpose line is **inside** the header element. The check-in card is still the header's `nextElementSibling` (the D-0195 / T-0471 pin).

### 2. Check-in card (UF-11.1, only while a proposal is pending)
- Behaviour, copy and writes are unchanged (`docs/specs/uf-11-plan-checkin.md`). It is restyled only:
  - `.wl-section` with label "Check-in";
  - the copy in body text;
  - the before → after preview as rows (`{Area}` left, `{current} → {next}` right, the numbers in DM Sans 700);
  - **Accept** as the primary button and **Keep current** as the secondary button, stacked full width.
- While this card is shown, its Accept is the one accent button on the screen. "Edit plan" (block 4) switches to the secondary style. See "Primary action".

### 3. Offline line
- The existing `OfflineStatus variant="text"`, unchanged, between the header (or check-in card) and the first card.

### 4. "Your plan" card
- `.wl-section`. Its `h2` is in label style: "Your plan".
- A `<dl>` with three rows in two columns (`dt` at least 96 px wide, `dd` takes the rest; 12 px row gap). Each `dt` is in caption style (13 px, `--wl-ink-muted`). Each `dd` is body text at weight 500.

| `dt` | `dd` line 1 | `dd` line 2 (caption, `--wl-ink-muted`) |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | "Build muscle" (goal label) | — |
| Rhythm | "3–5 per week" | "6–10 sessions per 14 days" |
| Priority areas | "Chest, Shoulders, Core" in the fixed area order, or "None" when there are none | — |

- Then **Edit plan** → `/plan/edit`: the primary button (`--wl-action` / `--wl-on-action`, min height 54 px, full card width). It is a link styled as a button.

### 5. Favorite exercises row (D-0202) and Excluded exercises row (D-0199)
- The Favorite exercises row sits directly **above** the Excluded exercises row, so the order is Your plan card, Favorite exercises, Excluded exercises, Targets. Its spec is under "Favorite exercises row" below.
- Directly under the "Your plan" card (so under the priority areas) and above the targets. The row's own spec follows in "Excluded exercises row" below, unchanged except that it now uses the shared `.wl-section` + `.wl-row` styles.

### 6. "Targets" card
- `.wl-section`. A label-style `h2` reads "Targets". On the same line, right-aligned, a caption: "Hard sets per 14 days".
- **A grid of nine tiles, not nine rows.** `<ul>` with `display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fill, minmax(88px, 1fr)); gap: 8px`. That gives 3 × 3 at 360–640 px widths and 2 columns under about 330 px (reflow at 320 px).
- Tiles appear in the fixed area order: chest, back, shoulders, arms, core, glutes, quads, hamstrings, calves.
- Each tile is an `<li>` with a `plan.raise` fill (the one place a filled surface stays), radius 12 px, padding 10 px 12 px and min height 72 px. It holds:
  - the area name: caption style, DM Sans 500 13 px, `plan.ink-on-raise` (4.9:1 on `plan.raise`). It wraps and is never truncated;
  - the target number: stat style, Big Shoulders Display 800 28 px, `--wl-ink`, with a visually hidden " hard sets" after it;
  - **at most one** extra caption line (12 px), shown **only when the source isn't the default**:
    - `source = adapted`: "Adapted {d MMM}" (existing copy);
    - `source = manual`: "Set by you" (existing copy);
    - `source = default`: **no line**. "From your plan" is no longer shown on any tile.
- **Priority areas:**
  - The tile has a 1 px `--wl-ink` border, and its caption line reads "Priority". The word carries the meaning, so colour is never the only signal.
  - If a priority area is also adapted or manual, the tile shows "Priority · Adapted 3 Oct".
- **Footnote** under the grid (caption, `--wl-ink-muted`), shown once: "Set from your goal, rhythm and priority areas." This replaces the nine repeated "From your plan" suffixes.
- **Link row** at the bottom of the card (`.wl-row`, 1 px `--wl-line` rule above): "See this period in Balance" → `/balance` (UF-10.1), with a chevron. This keeps "aim" (Plan) and "progress" (Balance) apart but one tap from each other. The link is unchanged from the build; the owner confirmed navigation additions on 2026-10-08 (H-32) and D-0203 §3 stands.
- The tiles have **no coverage colour.** C-01 colours mean "load against target this period". On Plan they would suggest progress, which this screen doesn't show. The C-01 body map was considered for this card and rejected for that reason.

### 7. "Check-ins" card
- `.wl-section`, with a label-style `h2`: "Check-ins".
- **Next date**, as a pair: a caption "First check-in" or "Next check-in" (D-0081: "First" before any period has ended and with no check-ins), then the date `{d MMM}` in stat style (Big Shoulders Display 800 28 px).
- **What happens** (secondary style, 14 px, `--wl-ink-muted`): "Every 14 days we compare your sessions with your rhythm. If you're under or over two periods in a row, we suggest a new rhythm. Nothing changes until you accept."
- **History** (the last 3, newest first), rows divided by 1 px `--wl-line`:
  - line 1, body weight 500: "{d MMM} · {from} → {to} per week";
  - line 2, caption `--wl-ink-muted`: "{n} sessions · {answer}", where answer is "Accepted", "Kept", "Withdrawn" or "Waiting for you".
  - With no check-ins: "No check-ins yet" (caption, `--wl-ink-muted`). There is no list.

### 8. "Routines" card
- `.wl-section`, with a label-style `h2`: "Routines".
- One `.wl-row` per routine, linking to `/plan/routines/{id}`:
  - the name (body, weight 500, wraps);
  - under it, the count caption "7 exercises";
  - a chevron at the right.
  - The accessible name is "{name}, 7 exercises".
- With no routines: "No routines yet" (caption, `--wl-ink-muted`).
- **New routine** → `/plan/routines/new`: the secondary button, full card width, with a 2 px stroke plus icon (`aria-hidden`).

### Removed from the screen
- The separate "Goal", "Rhythm", "Priority areas" and "Targets" `<h2>` blocks with loose paragraphs (prod).
- "From your plan" on every target row.
- The bottom "Edit plan" + "Account" button pair (D-0195 already moved Account to the header; Edit plan moves into block 4).

## Primary action

- **Edit plan** is the screen's one primary (accent) action. It sits in the "Your plan" card, next to the values it changes.
- **Exception:** while the check-in card is shown, its Accept is the only accent button. Edit plan then renders in the secondary style, in the same place and with the same label. This keeps one accent call to action per screen.
- Everything else (Account and sign out, Excluded exercises, See this period in Balance, routines, New routine) is a secondary button or a row link.

## States

| State | What renders |
|---|---|
| Loading (first read pending) | The header (title, Account and sign out, purpose line) only, plus a `role="status"` line "Loading your plan" (caption, `--wl-ink-muted`). No cards and no shimmer. |
| Cold cache (no plan on the device, offline) | The header, then the neutral notice pattern (`components/neutral-notice.md`) with "Your plan isn't on this device yet. Connect to load it." No cards. |
| Ready | Blocks 1 and 3–8. Block 2 appears only with a pending proposal. |
| Check-in pending | Block 2 shows, and Edit plan becomes secondary. |
| First period (no check-ins, no ended period) | Caption "First check-in"; history "No check-ins yet". |
| No priority areas | `dd` "None". No tile has the Priority treatment. Footnote unchanged. |
| Every target default | No tile has a source line, and the footnote explains the source once. |
| Some targets adapted / manual | Only those tiles show "Adapted {d MMM}" / "Set by you". |
| No routines | "No routines yet" + New routine. |
| Offline | Same as ready, plus the offline line. Every link still navigates; the destination screens disable their own writes. The check-in card disables its buttons (UF-11.1, unchanged). |
| Zero history / returning after 10 days off | Nothing special here. Targets come from the plan, and Balance shows the gap. |

## Accessibility

- Heading outline: `h1` Plan, then one `h2` per card: Check-in, Your plan, Targets, Check-ins, Routines. The Excluded exercises row is a link, not a heading.
- The targets grid is a `<ul aria-label="Targets, hard sets per 14 days">`. Each tile reads "Chest 29 hard sets", plus "Priority" or the source line when present.
- Contrast:
  - `--wl-ink` on `--wl-raise` is 14.8:1;
  - `--wl-ink-muted` on `--wl-raise` is 6.8:1 and on `--wl-raise` is 5.8:1;
  - `--wl-on-action` on `--wl-action` is 14.9:1.
  - The priority tile's `--wl-action` border is decorative, because the "Priority" text carries the meaning.
- Targets: every link, row and button is at least 44 px tall. Rows are 56 px, primary buttons 54 px and secondary buttons 48 px.
- Focus ring: 2 px `--wl-action`, 2 px offset, on every interactive element. Focus order follows the DOM order above.
- Reflow: at 320 px the grid drops to two columns and the header link wraps under the title. There is no horizontal scroll.
- Never shown during a workout. This is a tab screen; not UF-09. No UF-09 control is added.

## Acceptance criteria (for the web-feature:UF-11 build ticket)

1. The `h1` is in the header, together with the "Account and sign out" link and the purpose line. The check-in card is the header's `nextElementSibling` when a proposal is pending (vitest + RTL).
2. Ready state: exactly five `h2`s when a check-in is pending ("Check-in", "Your plan", "Targets", "Check-ins", "Routines") and four without it (vitest).
3. "From your plan" appears nowhere on the screen when every target is `default`. An adapted target's tile shows "Adapted {d MMM}" and a manual one shows "Set by you" (vitest, with fixtures that mix the three sources).
4. The targets list has nine `li`s in the fixed area order. Each accessible text matches `/^{Area} \d+ hard sets/`. Priority tiles contain "Priority" (vitest).
5. The Edit plan link has the primary class when no proposal is pending, and the secondary class when one is. In both cases exactly one element on the screen has the primary class (vitest).
6. The Excluded exercises row sits between the "Your plan" card and the "Targets" card, and keeps all its states below (vitest; T-0540's tests).
7. Playwright at 390 × 844: visual-foundation T-1 and G-2 hold on `/plan`. At 360 × 740 the targets grid is 3 columns (three tiles share a `top`); at 320 × 640 it is 2 columns with no horizontal scroll.
8. Playwright: every interactive element on `/plan` has a bounding box at least 44 px tall.
9. Existing tests that assert "From your plan" or the combined target/check-in row strings are updated to the new copy. This is a spec change, not a weakened test; the ticket log records each one.

## Excluded exercises row (D-0199, from T-0532; unchanged)

### Layout

- A row directly **under the priority areas** and above the per-area targets. In the reworked layout it sits between the "Your plan" card and the "Targets" card. It is a link (`<a href="/plan/excluded">`), the whole row is the target, at least 56 px tall and full width. A `--wl-raise` card with a 1 px `--wl-line` border.
- Text "Excluded exercises" (DM Sans 700, 16 px, `--wl-ink`) followed by the count part, with a 2 px stroke chevron icon at the right edge.
- Visible text: "Excluded exercises · 2" with n at least 1, and "Excluded exercises · none" when the list is empty. `--wl-ink-muted` for the "· 2" or "· none" part.
- The count is the stored list size, from this user's cache.

### States

| State | Row |
|---|---|
| Non-empty (n at least 1) | "Excluded exercises · 2" |
| Empty | "Excluded exercises · none". The row stays a link; UF-11.5 shows its empty state |
| Offline | Same as online. The row only navigates; it is not a write control, so it is not disabled |
| Loading, first run, no cache | "Excluded exercises" with no count until the first read resolves; no "none" is shown before then (D-0197 §2: only an authenticated empty read means none) |
| Count changes on return from UF-11.5 | The row shows the new count when UF-11.2 renders again |

### Accessibility

- Accessible name: "Excluded exercises, 2" or "Excluded exercises, none". The chevron is `aria-hidden`.
- Focus ring 2 px `--wl-action`, offset 2 px. Target at least 44 px (the row is 56 px).
- Never shown on UF-03, UF-08 or UF-09. No UF-09 control is added.
